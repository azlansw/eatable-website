Privacy Policy

Your allergy and health info is personal. Eatable keeps as little as it can, keeps most of it on your device, and never sells it.

The basics No ads. No selling or renting your data. No tracking cookies. Your food and reaction logs stay on your device.

What Eatable stores, and where

WhatWhere it lives
Your name, allergies, health conditions, foods to avoid, card notes, and emergency contactOn your device. If you make an account, also saved to your account (Supabase) so you can sign in on another device.
Your email and password (accounts only)Handled by Supabase's login system. Eatable never sees your password.
Food logs, reaction logs, and safe ordersOn your device only.
Friends' allergy cards you've addedOn your device, and on your account if you have one.
Saved menu checksOn your device for up to 3 days so repeat checks load fast.

Guest mode keeps everything on your device. If you clear your browser data or delete the app, guest data is gone.

What gets sent to other services

Some features need help from outside services. Eatable only sends what that feature needs:

  • Menu checks and AI setup (Google Gemini): the restaurant's name and address, plus a summary of what you avoid (your allergies and conditions). If you use the AI setup chat, your messages are sent too. AI requests go through Eatable's server so the AI key stays secret. Eatable doesn't store them there.
  • Barcode scans (Open Food Facts): just the barcode number.
  • Restaurant search and map (OpenStreetMap, Photon, Esri): your approximate location or the area you're looking at, so nearby places and map tiles can load. Eatable doesn't save your location.
  • Hosting (Vercel): like any website, the host may keep basic technical logs such as IP address and browser type.

Location and camera

Eatable only asks for your location to show nearby restaurants, and only uses the camera to scan barcodes. Both are optional, and you can turn them off in your browser or phone settings. Without location, the map starts in Valencia, CA.

Sharing your allergy card

Your card gets a share code. Anyone with that code or link can see what's on your card: your name, what you can't eat, notes, emergency contact, and safe orders. Nothing else is shared, including your logs.

Kids

Eatable is a student project and isn't directed at children under 13. If you're under 13, use it only with a parent or guardian. If a parent believes their child under 13 made an account, email us and we'll delete it.

Deleting your data

  • Guest: clear your browser's site data for Eatable, or delete the app.
  • Account: email azbusinessinquiries@gmail.com from your account's email address and your account and its saved profile will be deleted.

Security

Data travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS), and your account data is protected so only you can read it. No system is perfectly secure, so please don't put anything in Eatable you wouldn't want stored online.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change too.
